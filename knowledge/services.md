# Services Offered by Annu Jaswanth

Annu offers full-cycle engineering from concept and UI design to AI agent deployment and infrastructure.

## 1. Portfolio Websites
- **Description**: Hyper-personalized, ultra-modern portfolio websites designed to position founders, creators, software engineers, and executives at the top 1% of their field.
- **Key Features**: Sleek dark mode, interactive 3D/micro-animations, embedded AI representative (like RIO), contact & booking forms, SEO optimization, blazing fast performance (99+ Lighthouse scores).
- **Typical Turnaround**: 3–7 days.

## 2. Business Websites
- **Description**: High-converting commercial websites for startups, agencies, local businesses, and manufacturing/enterprise firms.
- **Key Features**: Clean conversion funnels, service showcase, responsive mobile-first layouts, integrated lead capture, dynamic blogs, multi-lingual support, analytics tracking.
- **Typical Turnaround**: 1–2 weeks.

## 3. AI Chatbots & Customer Assistants
- **Description**: Custom AI conversational agents trained specifically on company documentation, product catalogs, service menus, and FAQs.
- **Key Features**: Streaming responses, multi-modal capabilities, custom knowledge retrieval (RAG), lead capture triggers, conversational handoff, CRM synchronization.
- **Typical Turnaround**: 5–10 days.

## 4. Autonomous AI Agents
- **Description**: Multi-step AI reasoning agents capable of taking actions autonomously—browsing web sources, querying internal databases, executing function calls, sending notification emails, and scheduling appointments.
- **Key Features**: Tool-use (function calling), memory persistence, human-in-the-loop validation, multi-agent coordination, task orchestration.
- **Typical Turnaround**: 1–3 weeks.

## 5. Workflow Automation Systems
- **Description**: Connecting disparate business software (CRMs, Google Sheets, WhatsApp, Slack, Resend, Stripe) into cohesive automated event-driven pipelines.
- **Key Features**: Automated invoice generation, automated customer follow-ups, lead routing, webhook handlers, zero manual data entry.
- **Typical Turnaround**: 3–10 days.

## 6. Custom Dashboards & Admin Portals
- **Description**: Tailored internal analytics platforms and admin management consoles.
- **Key Features**: Role-based access control (RBAC), real-time charts and data visualization, CSV/PDF exports, CRUD operations, audit logging.
- **Typical Turnaround**: 1–3 weeks.

## 7. Full-Stack Web Applications (SaaS)
- **Description**: End-to-end production software with authentication, payment gateways (Stripe/Razorpay), relational databases, background tasks, and intuitive user interfaces.
- **Key Features**: Secure auth (Supabase/NextAuth/Clerk), subscription billing, tenant isolation, fast edge deployments.
- **Typical Turnaround**: 2–6 weeks.

## 8. Machine Learning & Computer Vision Solutions
- **Description**: Bespoke ML models for image classification, object detection, predictive analytics, and domain-specific dataset fine-tuning (similar to PestRisk & Veera RMC).
- **Key Features**: High-accuracy model training, edge or cloud inference pipelines, REST API deployment.
- **Typical Turnaround**: Scope-based (typically 2–4 weeks).
